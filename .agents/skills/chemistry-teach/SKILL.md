# Teach chemistry through conserved quantities and evidence

Read [homework-teach](../homework-teach/SKILL.md), its [lesson workflow](../homework-teach/references/lesson-workflow.md), and [science guidance](../homework-teach/references/science.md). They own privacy, routing, lesson structure, review, and delivery. This skill supplies chemistry decisions; it does not authorize experiments, lab conclusions, integration setup, or message sending. Route the new lesson as **Chemistry**; preserve legacy Science entries and existing URLs.

## Choose a coherent reasoning path

Inspect every supplied page visually, including notation, charge marks, arrows, units, and any handwritten work. Treat handwriting as uncertain learner reasoning, never as an answer key. Privately map the underlying demands to prerequisites, an original worked case, a transfer check, and any linked continuation. Do not publish that map or a source-question concordance.

Choose the smallest path that prepares the learner to act. Formula reading, electron accounting, spatial charge effects, and particle interactions are distinct tasks; a lesson about one does not silently certify the others. A lesson series may divide them, but the linked set must address the required reasoning. Say what information the learner still needs, such as a course convention, supplied geometry, or observed data.

Review separation at the level of the requested response. A generic definition, rank order, elemental list, or standard diagram can complete assigned work just as directly as a numerical answer. If so, teach its prerequisites or an independently different case and leave the final naming or inference to the learner. Change the reasoning context or representation as well as numbers: a token-counting model can introduce notation, but it must eventually connect to chemistry. A different atom symbol alone does not make a worked structure original.

Before teaching a procedure whose use depends on a particle model, identify the evidence that permits starting it. Distinguish selecting a representation, executing its method, and auditing its result. A task that names the model or supplies the decisive context does not exercise selecting it. For a consequential model choice, require a fresh case in which the learner chooses a defensible representation and explains the deciding fact, plus an insufficient-evidence case when a formula or inventory cannot settle that choice. Test the task without its method-naming heading: do the givens still support the decision? State the question and represented scale; allow compatible models or withheld judgment instead of imposing a universal two-category rule. Check that linked method summaries preserve this applicability decision rather than directing every formula straight into a molecular drawing.

## Build from a visible model to a method

Select the relevant branch rather than forcing every chemistry lesson through bonding:

- **Composition and quantities:** state what one counted unit is. Separate amount from composition, subscripts from coefficients, atoms from ions and molecules, and a molecular formula from an extended-solid ratio. Carry units through conversions; check dimensional consistency and sensible scale. For equation work, audit each element and total charge on both sides without balancing the assigned equation.
- **Electron structure and bonding:** distinguish a whole-diagram electron budget from a local shell check. When independent structure drawing is required, teach valence lookup, charge adjustment, a plausible skeleton, electron placement, shell audit, and revision on a genuinely new complete structure. An arithmetic ledger alone does not teach drawing. Exercise the starting decision as well: teach a bounded strategy for proposing connections, distinguish terminal atoms from possible centers, and require a fresh skeleton-choice check. Explain when supplied connectivity or additional evidence is necessary; do not imply every formula has one obvious structure. Explain why a shared pair counts once in the total and around both bonded atoms locally. State the range of any octet rule; unresolved formal charges, resonance, radicals, or expanded shells need an appropriate continuation rather than a forced simple answer.
- **Polarity and shape:** distinguish pair count, uneven sharing in one bond, and the spatial combination of effects in a whole particle. Electronegativity differences use one scale and are unitless; introductory cutoffs are approximate conventions. Obtain geometry from evidence or a stated shape model, not the placement of symbols on the page. If using arrows, verify directions, magnitudes, cancellation, and whether the illustration represents real chemistry or only vector addition.
- **Interactions, energy, and properties:** identify which particles interact and what changes. Separate internal connections from attractions between particles, physical change from chemical change, and trend from exact prediction. State comparison conditions such as pressure, temperature, size, and shape. Avoid universal force rankings and category-to-property shortcuts. Use synthetic data only when clearly labeled; never turn the learner's measurements into a lab conclusion.
- **Solutions, rates, and equilibrium:** identify species, amounts, concentration units, and the system boundary. Separate how fast change occurs from its favored extent; distinguish a model prediction from measured evidence. Define fixed conditions and audit conservation before interpreting a graph or changing an input.

Introduce unfamiliar language beside the visual, then formalize it. Prefer a small early diagnostic with a recovery explanation over assuming that a learner remembers a periodic-table convention. Place a new check immediately after each substantial concept; end with mixed decisions and an explanation task. Offer a useful pause point before a new model.

## Make the representation auditable

A diagram needs enough information to check the intended claim: clear atom identities or explicitly fictional labels, all relevant electrons or charges, visible units and axes, and a nearby text equivalent. Mark schematic sizes, supplied geometry, and simulated values. Do not let animation hide required electrons or manufacture missing evidence. Check initial, intermediate, final, paused, reset, reduced-motion, and no-script states. A fixed successful example should remain readable without running motion.

Match transfer to the kind of work the goal asks the learner to produce. After teaching a complete construction, require a new complete construction with an independent audit, not just recognition of the demonstrated repair. After using fictional tokens to introduce notation, include a genuinely new authentic representation that the learner must translate; a worked bridge alone is not transfer. A scratch-paper attempt with a closed, accessible comparison is sufficient when a drawing widget would add needless complexity. Compare each prediction and transfer prompt against all preceding revealed content, including diagrams and numbered worked steps. Place the prediction before its outcome is visible, and specify exactly which quantities might change or stay fixed. A changed ratio that asks for an already displayed conclusion is still recognition; use a new arrangement, reverse task, or construction to require a new decision. Audit every choice set and the whole page for plausible distractors, balanced wording, and varied correct positions. After replacing a task, regenerate its hint ladder and comparison as one unit; audit every referenced object, count, direction, and requested action against the new givens. For a rule with multiple necessary conditions, supply a new case that requires checking each condition. Include near misses that satisfy one condition but not another, and give condition-specific feedback. Recalling the rule or naming evidence to inspect does not replace applying it to supplied evidence.

Practice must diagnose a plausible error and permit retry. Keep controls local to the page; prevent native form submission when scripts fail or are unavailable. Each component may enable only the forms whose feedback handlers it has attached. Hide a static comparison only when that specific form is ready, not because another script enhanced the page. When numeric practice and chemistry models use separate scripts, block each script independently as well as disabling all JavaScript. Confirm that unavailable checks stay disabled, their comparisons remain readable, working components still give feedback, and pressing Enter puts no response into a URL or request. Test that visible and accessible diagram descriptions match the current state, and that static/print teaching survives collapsed disclosures.

## Verify the full learning route

Define the learner's next reading or lookup action separately from the author's verification need before choosing a source. Prefer the smallest complete destination that serves that action. A periodic table can support symbol and group lookup without teaching valence counting; describe that limited role honestly. Use a dated reference only for facts appropriate to that edition, and keep the lesson's core method readable without requiring a dense external document. Add original local prerequisite material only when a suitable external route cannot serve the learner; local prose is not independent evidence.

Match author evidence to the claim: consult the issuing authority for a definition, measured reference data for a physical quantity with temperature and pressure, and an independent calculation or boundary case for a mathematical relationship. Internally consistent arithmetic does not validate a supplied physical datum. For main-group shortcuts, include an ordinary case and meaningful exceptions such as helium or filled inner shells. Keep broad author-verification references separate from the learner's next action.

Inspect each complete learner-facing destination: substantive text, figures, accessible descriptions, worked examples, reveals, and required embedded material. A section anchor does not remove the rest of a page from review. Test prominent claims, conditions, units, and representation consistency as well as the cited paragraph; privately compare the destination with every supplied response demand. A reputable publisher is not proof of page correctness or separation. Replace a destination with a demonstrated misleading claim or unresolved material datum rather than pointing only to its correct portion. Apply any strengthened source check to every retained destination in the linked sequence, not just the changed link. Record its exact URL/edition, intended role, observed checks, and retrieval or accessibility limits. Recheck locally authored reference pages too; no publisher-wide approval or historical score replaces this route-level exercise.

Use [the repository rubric](../../../docs/REVIEW-RUBRIC.md) for requested independent review. Provide a fixed candidate and all loaded dependencies, the authorized private source, and the declared lesson/series scope, without an author's score. Assess both each page and the linked coverage. Apply findings to the reusable chemistry instructions when they expose a recurring generation failure, then test that changed guidance against the next candidate. Preserve real scores, evidence limitations, exact hashes, and pending delivery; a target is not a reason to inflate an assessment.
