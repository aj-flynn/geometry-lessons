# English

Apply [homework-teach](../homework-teach/SKILL.md). Map the reading or writing decisions and the required response. Preserve the interpretation demands of voice, implied versus stated attitudes, evidence density and qualifications in independent practice. Use unrelated language, passages, imagery, characters and claims.

- Separate observation, plain meaning and interpretation. Check each paraphrase against its wording for actor, action, timing, frequency, negation, uncertainty and conditions. Distinguish speaker, character, narrator and author. Find the smallest useful quotation in a fresh unmarked passage with comparable evidence density. Include a plausible but insufficient detail or rival reading so the choice requires interpreting the wording. Explain how evidence supports a bounded claim, and show how to repair a response that quotes or retells without making that connection.
- When translating older language, teach needed pronouns, verbs and word order with new lines, then bridge literal meaning to natural English. Preserve linked or mixed imagery across clauses when that is part of the required interpretation. In dialogue analysis, connect the wording to a speaker's stance toward the other person and test it against their response. Use genuine details to build tempting alternatives.
- Match the requested claim and genre. For a two-source argument, open with complete original readings in comparable forms. Preserve abstract claims and qualifications when the source uses them. Include plausible details whose value requires interpretation. Before teaching the strategy, ask for one detail from each and a provisional claim. Ask what the second reading changes or establishes that the first could not. Then reveal an annotated model that introduces and attributes evidence, explains both roles and revises the claim. End with the independent writing the task requires.
- Keep relevant passages readily available during decisions. Preserve ordinary reading flow for narrative, extended reading, and writing; use a split when compact source analysis benefits from repeated reference. Group a marked quotation, explanatory note and caption in one figure. Move from annotation to an uncued attempt. Verify exact quotation, grammar and the evidence link. Check mobile/print reading and visible, contrasting keyboard controls. Give a bounded way to check the learner's own work privately.
