# Science concepts

Apply the [homework-teach contract](../homework-teach/SKILL.md), then continue here without restarting subject routing. Read its [science guidance](../homework-teach/references/science.md).

Use this branch for broad or integrated science. For chemistry, load [chemistry-teach](../chemistry-teach/SKILL.md); for an unsupported distinct discipline, use the shared new-subject workflow. Preserve existing Science manifest records and URLs unless intentionally reclassifying their public subject.

Identify what is observed, what is inferred, and what the model assumes. Teach quantities and units before equations. Check conservation, dimensions, signs, axes, valid ranges, and boundary cases. Prefer a concrete labeled model, then a relationship, then a fresh transfer check. Treat synthetic data as simulated, and leave the learner's lab conclusion and actual measurements unanalyzed.

Choose sources appropriate to the discipline and verify the exact claims. A visual model illustrates a relationship; it is not experimental evidence. Use controlled comparisons with a stated variable, fixed conditions, and limitations. Feedback should address the reason a plausible misconception fails, followed by a different retry. Evaluate the resulting lesson and this process with the shared independent-refinement workflow.
