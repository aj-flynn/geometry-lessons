# Mission: Work through homework independently

## Why
Learn the geometry and science concepts needed to approach homework independently. Build understanding through freshman-level explanations, a glossary, accurate diagrams, controllable motion, and practice with different examples.

## Success looks like
- Recognize the relevant relationship and explain why it applies.
- Choose a method, justify each step, and check whether a result makes sense.
- Explain a scientific relationship, read its units, and recognize what a model assumes or leaves out.
- Return to a short lesson or reference when a concept needs refreshing.

## Constraints
- Never give answers to actual homework, confirm a proposed homework answer, or publish its worked solution.
- Public materials contain only generalized concepts and genuinely different examples.
- Introduce unfamiliar terms in plain language and collect them in a glossary.
- Keep input photos, PDFs, Word documents, extracted text, personal details, and learner records outside this repository and its history.

## Out of scope
Answer keys, graded-work completion, lab-report completion, student profiles, analytics, public uploads, and inbox automation setup.
