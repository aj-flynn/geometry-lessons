# Teaching resources

- [Poetry Foundation: Glossary of Poetic Terms](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/education/glossary): reference definitions for metaphor, simile, personification, and hyperbole. English lesson examples are original.
- [Folger Shakespeare Library: Reading Shakespeare's Language](https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/shakespeares-sonnets/reading-shakespeares-language-sonnets/): historical word meanings, unusual word order, and sentence structure. No source passages are reproduced in the English lesson.

- [Matt Pocock's teach skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md): source of the mission-led, short HTML lesson, reusable asset, retrieval practice, and feedback approach. Adapted here for public concept lessons without private learning records or homework answers. MIT notice: `skills/geometry-teach/LICENSE-MATT-POCOCK.txt`.

- [University of Waterloo CEMC: Similarity and Congruence](https://courseware.cemc.uwaterloo.ca/46/assignments/1239/5): primary educational reference for the similarity conditions, a consistent scale direction, and perimeter and area scaling. Used to verify the relationships in the similar-polygons lesson; its exercises and illustrations were not copied.
- [Math Open Reference: Similar Polygons](https://www.mathopenref.com/similarpolygons.html): corroborating reference for ordered correspondence, rotation/reflection, and the two similarity conditions.


- [Shakespeare’s Words: Language Companion](https://www.shakespeareswords.com/Public/LanguageCompanion/TopicList.aspx): reference for older pronoun and verb forms, contractions, and context-dependent vocabulary in the English toolkit.
