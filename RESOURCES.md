# Teaching resources

- [Pasco-Hernando State College Writing Center: Figures of Speech](https://writing-center.phsc.edu/writing/literature/fiction/fiction-figures-speech): accessible institutional reference for figurative-language distinctions. The full destination was reviewed for accuracy and separation; English lesson examples remain original.
- [Folger Shakespeare Library: Reading Shakespeare's Language](https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/shakespeares-sonnets/reading-shakespeares-language-sonnets/): historical word meanings, unusual word order, and sentence structure. No source passages are reproduced in the English lesson.

- [Matt Pocock's teach skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md): source of the mission-led, short HTML lesson, reusable asset, retrieval practice, and feedback approach. Adapted here for public concept lessons without private learning records or homework answers. [MIT notice](.agents/skills/homework-teach/LICENSE-MATT-POCOCK.txt).

- [Illustrative Mathematics: What are scaled copies?](https://im.kendallhunt.com/MS/students/2/1/1/index.html): visual scaling and a consistent multiplier across corresponding lengths.
- [Math Open Reference: Similar triangles and area](https://www.mathopenref.com/similartrianglesareas.html): focused interactive comparison of length factors and area factors. Active learner-facing geometry sources were narrowed after independent review; earlier broad destinations are retained only in historical review records.


- [Shakespeare’s Words: Language Companion](https://www.shakespeareswords.com/Public/LanguageCompanion/TopicList.aspx): reference for older pronoun and verb forms, contractions, and context-dependent vocabulary in the English toolkit.


## Chemistry concepts

- [OpenStax Chemistry 2e: Chemical formulas](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/2-4-chemical-formulas): composition versus structure and particle counting.
- [Lewis symbols and structures](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-3-lewis-symbols-and-structures): electron budgets and model limitations.
- [Ionic bonding](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-1-ionic-bonding): charged particles, electron transfer, and extended structures.
- [Covalent bonding](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-2-covalent-bonding) and [Molecular structure and polarity](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-6-molecular-structure-and-polarity): electron-pull differences, approximate conventions, and geometry-dependent combined effects.
- [Intermolecular forces](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/10-1-intermolecular-forces), [Properties of liquids](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/10-2-properties-of-liquids), and [The solid state](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/10-5-the-solid-state-of-matter): conditional property trends and limits of particle models.

Additional sources verified during subject-skill regeneration:

- [IUPAC periodic table, 4 May 2022 edition](https://iupac.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/IUPAC_Periodic_Table-04May22_CRA.pdf): learner lookup of element symbols, names, and numbered groups. This dated one-page PDF is not evidence for valence counts or a recommendation of current atomic-weight values.
- [NIST carbon atomic data](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/carbontable1.htm), [helium atomic data](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/heliumtable1.htm), and [gallium atomic data](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/galliumtable1.htm): author verification of neutral ground-state outer-shell counts, the helium exception, and the distinction between filled inner d orbitals and the outer shell. These spectroscopy tables are author evidence, not beginner reading assignments.
- [Royal Society of Chemistry: Atomic data](https://periodic-table.rsc.org/Help/AtomicData): meanings and limits of tabulated atomic quantities.
- [Math Is Fun: Vectors](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/vectors.html): direction-dependent addition and subtraction, with representative calculations independently checked during source review.
- [NIST Chemistry WebBook: normal-boiling-point measurements](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C74986&Type=TBOIL): optional practice reading measured values with units, provenance, and uncertainty. These data do not establish a force-name ranking or a controlled comparison between substances.

The chemistry sequence uses original prerequisite models and independently selected worked structures and transfer cases. The printable field guide links the four lessons and their glossaries. Numerical classification cutoffs are left to the learner's course convention; no assignment-specific label map or element list is supplied. This resource list records author verification; each lesson's learner-facing source selection is reviewed separately against its supplied input.
