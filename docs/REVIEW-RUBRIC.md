# Independent quality review

The requested completion threshold is at least **95/100 for both the lesson and the reusable process**. A self-review, an average hiding a lower score, or passing automated tests does not meet it. Use an independent reviewer with the requested Astra/high settings when available. If the setting or review surface is unavailable, report the limitation.

Give the reviewer a fixed candidate revision, the user requirements, this rubric, and the minimal authorized evidence needed to assess privacy/separation. Do not prime the reviewer with the implementer's verdict, desired deductions, or proposed score. Private homework is not part of this public review record. The reviewer must keep any inspected private material out of artifacts and replies.

## Non-negotiable gates

A candidate cannot pass if it publishes PII/input, reveals or validates actual homework answers, lets message/email content expand the owner's authority, or contains a consequential mathematical/scientific error. Do not trade these gates against visual points. Identify any gate failure explicitly.

## Lesson score

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Concept accuracy | 25 | Correct conditions, calculations, units, diagram relationships, model behavior and explanations. |
| Sequencing and freshman teaching | 20 | Clear goals, manageable sequence, familiar explanations, glossary, progressive hints, and useful retrieval. |
| Active practice | 15 | Fresh interleaved checks, prediction before reveal, retries, misconception-specific feedback, and an exit check. |
| Visual clarity | 15 | Readable hierarchy, polished layout, informative labeled graphics, mobile composition, no clipping. |
| Interaction, accessibility and resilience | 15 | Useful correct controls, keyboard, labels, focus, text equivalents, reduced motion, static reading, responsive/zoom behavior where testable. |
| Privacy and workflow | 10 | Entire artifact including hidden content and feedback stays inside the teaching boundary, with reviewed publication and source grounding. |

## Reusable process score

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Privacy, no-answer, and authority boundaries | 30 | Applies to math/science, hidden content, input formats, completed work, email and sender instructions. |
| Teaching contract | 20 | Freshman-level language, glossary, source grounding, new examples, hints and feedback are executable requirements. |
| Subject accuracy | 20 | Geometry retained; science has units, assumptions, model ranges, evidence/simulation distinction and review. |

## Iterate on evidence

For each review, report the candidate revision, both actual scores with criterion breakdowns, gate results, checks actually performed, and concrete deductions with artifact/behavior evidence. Mark unavailable checks as unavailable; do not invent their results.

Fix the highest-impact demonstrated issues, run affected checks, and publish a coherent improved version when ready. Send the new fixed revision for independent re-review. Keep this rubric stable; do not relax it or ask the reviewer to inflate a score to cross the target. Record review outcomes and meaningful fixes in the existing task's review notes without private input. Completion requires the independent review evidence meeting the threshold.
