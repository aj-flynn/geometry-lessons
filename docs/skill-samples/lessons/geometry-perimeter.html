Job 1 · The same tag, two jobs

Measure around or cover the face

The tag is a rectangle, a four-sided shape with four square corners. It is 4 centimeters (cm) wide and 2 cm tall.

A metal rim goes along the boundary, the outside edge. Its total length is the perimeter. A stamp covers the inside. The space it covers is the area. One square centimeter (cm²) is the space inside a square 1 cm on each side.

Predict. Which marks will you count for the rim? Which marks will you count for the stamp? Point before you check.

Compare the two jobs

For the rim, trace the four outside sides. For the stamp, count the eight squares inside.

Cut the rim

Find the length of metal for one loop. Start at the top and trace clockwise, the way a clock hand moves. Count each side once.

4 cm2 cm4 cm2 cm
  1. Match the sides across from each other.
  2. The top and bottom together measure 4 cm + 4 cm = 8 cm.
  3. The left and right together measure 2 cm + 2 cm = 4 cm.
  4. Add both pairs. 8 cm + 4 cm = 12 cm around.

Cover the tag

Find how much space the stamp covers. Fit the squares together with no gaps or overlaps.

  1. Each tile covers 1 cm².
  2. There are 2 rows, with 4 tiles in each row.
  3. Multiply to find the area. 2 × 4 cm² = 8 cm².

The 2 counts rows. The 4 cm² measures one row.

Try it · A cart’s loop

How far does the cart roll in one loop?

Compare the loop

In meters, 5 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 5 + 3 = 20. The cart rolls 20 m. A square inside is a different thing from one short edge on the route.

To retry, start from a different corner. Will the total change?

Try it · A patch for a toy

How much cloth covers this whole patch?

Compare the patch

Two full rows and one extra tile give 3 cm² + 3 cm² + 1 cm² = 7 cm².

To retry, count down the columns. Check that you get the same area.

Good place to pause. Point to what you count for each job before you move on.

Job 2 · The whole loop is known

Find what is missing

One piece left

Given a triangle with perimeter 24 cm. Two pieces are 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the length of the last piece.

  1. Add the known pieces. 6 cm + 8 cm = 14 cm.
  2. Take them out of the total. 24 cm − 14 cm = 10 cm.
  3. Check by putting the piece back. 14 cm + 10 cm = 24 cm.

Two matching pieces left

Given a rectangle with perimeter 22 cm. The left side is 3 cm. Find the other three lengths.

  1. Across from 3 cm is another 3 cm.
  2. Both short sides take 3 cm + 3 cm = 6 cm.
  3. The two long sides have 22 cm − 6 cm = 16 cm left.
Top ?Bottom ?

16 cm left for these two equal lengths, or lengths that match

  1. Share it equally. 16 cm ÷ 2 = 8 cm per long side.
  2. Check by adding. 3 cm + 8 cm + 3 cm + 8 cm = 22 cm.

Try it · Order a new strip

A maker has a 27 cm loop made from four straight pieces. Three pieces measure 5 cm, 7 cm and 9 cm. How long is the fourth? Show how to check it with addition.

Compare the new strip

5 cm + 7 cm + 9 cm = 21 cm. Then 27 cm − 21 cm = 6 cm. Check that 21 cm + 6 cm = 27 cm.

If you got stuck, sketch four pieces in a strip. Label what you know and leave one gap. Try again.

Good place to pause. Tell someone why the frame needed an extra step after subtraction.

Job 3 · Every piece matches

Share the whole loop

Given a 28 cm loop with four equal pieces. Find the length of one piece.

????

28 cm for all four pieces

  1. Share the whole length. 28 cm ÷ 4 = 7 cm per piece.
  2. Put the pieces back. 4 × 7 cm = 28 cm.

Try it · Which cutting mark?

Five equal bars join end to end to make a loop. Together they measure 45 cm. Where should the maker mark the length for one bar?

Compare your mark

45 cm ÷ 5 = 9 cm, so mark 9 cm. Check that 5 × 9 cm = 45 cm. A mark at 5 cm uses the number of bars as a length.

To retry, draw five equal boxes and write 45 cm over the whole row. Find what belongs in just one box.

Your turn · Pick your method

Three new orders

For each order, write a number with its unit. Circle or say the clue that helped you choose your first step.

  1. Tray. Three rows of 5 foam squares fit the base with no gaps. Each square is 1 cm on a side. A clear sheet must fit the base, and a cord must fit around the edge. How much sheet and how much cord?
  2. Robot badge. A rectangle has 30 cm of trim all the way around it. Its two long sides use 18 cm together. What is the length of each short side?
  3. Signal sign. A triangle is made from three equal strips. A 33 cm roll makes one whole outline. Where would you make the first cut if no length is lost at the cut?
Compare after you try all three
  1. Tray. The sheet covers space. One row covers 5 cm². Three rows cover 3 × 5 cm² = 15 cm². The cord goes around. One row is 5 cm long. Three rows make the tray 3 cm tall. Add the four sides. 5 cm + 3 cm + 5 cm + 3 cm = 16 cm.
  2. Robot badge. “All the way around” gives the perimeter. The long sides already use 18 cm together. The short pair has 30 cm − 18 cm = 12 cm left. Each short side is 12 cm ÷ 2 = 6 cm. Check by adding. 18 cm + 6 cm + 6 cm = 30 cm.
  3. Signal sign. “Three equal strips” tells you how many shares. 33 cm ÷ 3 = 11 cm per strip. Cut at 11 cm. Check by multiplying. 3 × 11 cm = 33 cm.

Try again on paper. Say what your unit measures before doing any arithmetic.

Tomorrow, before you look. Sketch a shape made of six tiles. How could you find the distance around it? How could you find the space it covers? Explain why the units differ.

When you go back to your work, point to the part being measured. For a missing length, add your pieces back and see if they fit the given total.

Glossary

Rectangle
A four-sided shape with four square corners. The sides across from each other have the same length.
Centimeter (cm)
A unit of length. One small tile edge in the tag measures 1 cm.
Boundary
The outside edge of the shape.
Perimeter
The total length all the way around the boundary.
Area
How much flat space a shape covers. Measure it in square units.
Square centimeter (cm²)
The area of a square that is 1 cm on each side. Say “square centimeter.”
Meter (m)
A unit of length. One meter is 100 centimeters.
Equal lengths
Lengths that measure the same. Matching little marks on sides show equal lengths.
