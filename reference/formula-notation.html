Formula and state notation

Read 2 Mg3(PO4)2(s) from the inside out. Start with one formula unit of magnesium phosphate, a solid.

  1. One PO4 group has 1 P and 4 O.
  2. Two PO4 groups contribute 2 P and 8 O. The Mg3 contributes 3 Mg to the same unit.
  3. The first 2 doubles the whole unit. The counts are 6 Mg, 4 P and 16 O.

The letters in parentheses after the formula show the state. They have no effect on the atom count.

(s)
Solid
(l)
Liquid
(g)
Gas
(aq)
Dissolved in water
