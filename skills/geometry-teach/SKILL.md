# Geometry teaching

Read and follow [homework-teach](../homework-teach/SKILL.md), the canonical workflow shared with science. Route geometry requests to its Geometry branch. Keep the same repository, existing lesson URLs, geometry components, privacy rules, and no-homework-answer boundary.

The [shared lesson workflow](../homework-teach/references/lesson-workflow.md) defines review, metadata, glossary, index, publication, and delivery. Existing `math` reviews remain compatible; new version-2 entries also attest to subject `accuracy`.

The [DOCX text-inspection helper](scripts/read-docx.py) remains at its existing path and still requires visual review of diagrams and equations. [Matt Pocock's MIT notice](LICENSE-MATT-POCOCK.txt) is preserved.
