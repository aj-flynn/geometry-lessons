How to use a lesson

  1. 1

    Read it together

    Each step is short, so a grown-up can read it out loud. Look at the picture for each step.

  2. 2

    Try it with your hands

    Use things you can touch, like blocks, coins or crayons, to show the idea. Tap “Why?” to hear the reason.

  3. 3

    Do your own work

    Try the practice with the hints. Then go back to your own page and do it yourself.
