How to use a lesson

  1. 1

    Work through the example

    Follow the worked example one step at a time. In geometry lessons, click through the slideshow for each kind of problem as its diagram gets marked up.

  2. 2

    Ask why

    If a step feels like magic, look for its reason: the “Why?” under each geometry step, or a hint. Underlined words open the glossary.

  3. 3

    Try a fresh one

    Each lesson has its own practice with hints. Check your thinking there, then go back to your own work.
