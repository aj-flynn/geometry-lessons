A working authoring specimen: count the chosen signal

A fictional beacon repeats one long flash and two short flashes in each complete cycle. Before checking, predict how many short flashes occur in four complete cycles. Explain what should be counted and what should be left out.

Notice what repeats

Count only the requested kind of flash in one cycle, then use the number of complete cycles.

Compare your reasoning, including without scripts

Each complete cycle contributes two short flashes, so four cycles give 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8 short flashes. Twelve counts all flashes, and two counts only one cycle. Retry with a different case: three complete cycles and five short flashes per cycle. Predict before opening the next comparison.

Compare the retry

Three groups of five short flashes give fifteen. The method depends on complete cycles and a fixed pattern; part of a cycle needs information about where it stops.
